一平方米的文明
The Civilization of
One Square Meter

一座以桌面为对象的内容档案库，收录文学意象、经典艺术、社会素材与形式灵感四个分类共 200 个词条，沿时间线索与桌面类型学两条路径，追问一张约一平方米的平面如何承载仪式、知识、权力、技术与记忆。展厅中的每一块 1㎡ 网格在这里拥有对应的词条，而每一个词条，都将在展厅中找到自己的位置。

A growing archive of the desk, housing 200 entries across four collections—Literary Imagery, Classic Art Archive, Social Materials, and Formal Inspiration. Along a timeline and a typology of desks, it asks how a surface of roughly one square meter carries ritual, knowledge, power, technology, and memory. Every 1㎡ grid module in the gallery has its corresponding entry here, and every entry will find its place in the gallery.
